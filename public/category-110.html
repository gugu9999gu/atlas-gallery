Distribution & statistics

Open an entry for its visual reference, comparisons and source-language text.

Histogram 히스토그램

Bins quantitative observations and shows the frequency per interval.

Use when designing distribution & statistics and selecting analytical visualizations.

Unlike categorical bars, bin widths and boundaries affect interpretation.

Reference

Box Plot 상자수염 그림

Summarizes a median, quartiles, whiskers and outliers.

Use when designing distribution & statistics and selecting analytical visualizations.

Specify whether whiskers are min–max or follow the 1.5 IQR rule.

Reference

Violin Plot 바이올린 플롯

Uses a symmetric density silhouette to show a distribution.

Use when designing distribution & statistics and selecting analytical visualizations.

Width represents estimated density, not raw counts.

Reference

Density Plot 밀도 곡선

Shows an estimated probability density as a continuous curve.

Use when designing distribution & statistics and selecting analytical visualizations.

Smoothing choices can change the apparent number of modes.

Reference

ECDF Plot 경험적 누적분포

Shows the proportion of observations at or below each value.

Use when designing distribution & statistics and selecting analytical visualizations.

The vertical axis is cumulative proportion, not individual frequency.

Reference

Strip Plot 스트립 플롯

Places individual observations along a quantitative axis.

Use when designing distribution & statistics and selecting analytical visualizations.

Use jitter or a beeswarm layout to reduce overlap.

Reference

Beeswarm Plot 비스웜 플롯

Offsets points to avoid overlap while preserving quantitative position.

Use when designing distribution & statistics and selecting analytical visualizations.

The perpendicular offset is not a separate variable.

Reference

Ridgeline Plot 리지라인 플롯

Stacks partially overlapping distributions for multiple groups.

Use when designing distribution & statistics and selecting analytical visualizations.

Excessive overlap can hide distribution differences.

Reference

Error Bar Chart 오차 막대 차트

Adds uncertainty intervals around point estimates.

Use when designing distribution & statistics and selecting analytical visualizations.

Define whether intervals are confidence intervals, standard errors or deviations.

Reference

Confidence Band 신뢰구간 밴드

Shows an uncertainty band around an estimated line.

Use when designing distribution & statistics and selecting analytical visualizations.

Confidence intervals and prediction intervals are not interchangeable.

Reference
