Composition & hierarchy

Open an entry for its visual reference, comparisons and source-language text.

Pie Chart 파이 차트

Shows parts of a whole through sector angles.

Use when designing composition & hierarchy and selecting analytical visualizations.

Sectors extend to the center, unlike a donut chart.

Reference

Donut Chart 도넛 차트

Shows composition as sectors around a hollow center.

Use when designing composition & hierarchy and selecting analytical visualizations.

Represents multiple parts of a whole, not progress of a single task.

Reference

Treemap 트리맵

Encodes hierarchical values as nested rectangle areas.

Use when designing composition & hierarchy and selecting analytical visualizations.

Consider label legibility and the limitations of area comparison.

Reference

Waffle Chart 와플 차트

Fills equal-sized grid cells to show proportions.

Use when designing composition & hierarchy and selecting analytical visualizations.

With 100 cells, each cell is 1%; document rounding.

Reference

Sunburst Chart 선버스트 차트

Encodes hierarchy depth as concentric rings and composition as angles.

Use when designing composition & hierarchy and selecting analytical visualizations.

Shows proportions within a level and parent–child relationships.

Reference

Icicle Chart 아이시클 차트

Stacks rectangular layers to show hierarchical depth.

Use when designing composition & hierarchy and selecting analytical visualizations.

A Cartesian alternative to a sunburst, with children inside parent widths.

Reference

Circle Packing 원 패킹

Uses nested circles to show containment in a hierarchy.

Use when designing composition & hierarchy and selecting analytical visualizations.

Gaps mean the layout is not a space-filling pie-like partition.

Reference
