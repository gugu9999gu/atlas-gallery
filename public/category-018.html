Bubble Chart 버블 차트

Use when designing relationships & multivariate and selecting analytical visualizations.

Scale area, not radius, proportionally to the value.

Reference

Connected Scatterplot 연결 산점도

Connects observations in time order across two quantitative axes.

Use when designing relationships & multivariate and selecting analytical visualizations.

Provide explicit direction and time ordering.

Reference

Correlation Matrix 상관행렬

Displays pairwise correlation coefficients in a symmetric matrix.

Use when designing relationships & multivariate and selecting analytical visualizations.

The diagonal is self-correlation; distinguish sign from strength.

Reference

Scatterplot Matrix 산점도 행렬

Arranges pairwise variable combinations as small scatterplots.

Use when designing relationships & multivariate and selecting analytical visualizations.

Unlike a correlation matrix, it shows observation-level distributions.

Reference

Parallel Coordinates 평행좌표

Represents each observation as a line crossing parallel variable axes.

Use when designing relationships & multivariate and selecting analytical visualizations.

Axis scales and ordering affect perceived patterns.

Reference

Radar Chart 레이더 차트

Places multiple measures on radial axes and connects their values.

Use when designing relationships & multivariate and selecting analytical visualizations.

Polygon area changes with axis order; do not treat it as a score.
