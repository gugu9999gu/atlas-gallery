Renderers, Trails, Ribbons & Decals

Open an entry for its visual reference, comparisons and source-language text.

Decal 데칼

Visual information projected onto or overlaid on another surface.

Bullet marks, footprints, and ground magic circles.

This is usually surface decoration rather than an object with its own thickness.

Reference

Trail 트레일·이동 궤적

A visual trace left along the path of a moving object or particle.

Sword arcs, rocket smoke, and light trails.

A trail describes a result or behavior; it need not be implemented as a ribbon.

Reference

Sprite Particle 스프라이트 입자

A particle represented by a plane carrying a 2D image.

Fire, smoke, and sparkles.

Particle data and visible geometry are separate. The same particles can be rendered as sprites, meshes, ribbons, or other forms.

Reference

Billboard 빌보드

A plane aligned to face the camera or another specified direction.

Particles, trees, and nameplates.

Particle data and visible geometry are separate. The same particles can be rendered as sprites, meshes, ribbons, or other forms.

Reference

Stretched Billboard 늘어난 빌보드

A planar particle stretched according to its velocity or orientation.

Rain streaks, sparks, and speed lines.

Particle data and visible geometry are separate. The same particles can be rendered as sprites, meshes, ribbons, or other forms.

Reference

Mesh Particle 메시 입자

Representing each particle with a 3D mesh.

Rocks, debris, and three-dimensional magical fragments.

Particle data and visible geometry are separate. The same particles can be rendered as sprites, meshes, ribbons, or other forms.

Reference

Ribbon 리본

Rendering connected points as a continuous strip of geometry.

Trails, electricity, and magical ribbons.

A ribbon can also create stationary curves and connections, so it is not exactly synonymous with a trail.

Reference

Beam 빔

A line, strip, or volumetric effect connecting a start and end point.

Lasers, lightning, and connecting energy.

Particle data and visible geometry are separate. The same particles can be rendered as sprites, meshes, ribbons, or other forms.

Reference

Ribbon Width Curve 리본 폭 곡선

A rule varying ribbon width along its length or according to age.

Tapering tails.

Particle data and visible geometry are separate. The same particles can be rendered as sprites, meshes, ribbons, or other forms.

Reference

Trail Lifetime 트레일 유지 시간

The duration for which each segment of a trail remains visible.

Short sword trails and long afterimages.

Particle data and visible geometry are separate. The same particles can be rendered as sprites, meshes, ribbons, or other forms.

Reference

Trail UV Mapping 트레일 UV 매핑

The arrangement of texture coordinates along a ribbon or trail.

Flowing light and repeating patterns.

Particle data and visible geometry are separate. The same particles can be rendered as sprites, meshes, ribbons, or other forms.

Reference

Projected Decal 투영 데칼

Projecting an image onto surfaces within a specified spatial volume.

Marks on walls and terrain.

Particle data and visible geometry are separate. The same particles can be rendered as sprites, meshes, ribbons, or other forms.

Reference

Mesh Decal 메시 데칼

Displaying a decal through a separate mesh placed over a surface.

Detailed surface patterns.

Particle data and visible geometry are separate. The same particles can be rendered as sprites, meshes, ribbons, or other forms.

Reference

Soft Particle 소프트 파티클

A particle treatment that softens intersections with scene surfaces.

Smoke intersecting the ground.

Particle data and visible geometry are separate. The same particles can be rendered as sprites, meshes, ribbons, or other forms.

Reference

Volumetric Effect 볼류메트릭 효과

An effect representing density or scattering throughout space rather than only on a plane.

Fog, smoke, and light shafts.

Particle data and visible geometry are separate. The same particles can be rendered as sprites, meshes, ribbons, or other forms.

Reference

Particle Light 입자 연동 조명

A light linked to a particle's position or attributes.

Light cast around fire and explosions.

An emissive material does not automatically illuminate its surroundings.

Reference

Geometry Trail 기하 트레일

A movement trail created by accumulating or deforming actual geometry.
